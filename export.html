How an export order runs

Five steps from enquiry to container.

  1. Enquiry

    Tell us the market, the products and the monthly volume you are planning. We reply with availability and indicative pricing.

  2. Sampling

    A sample carton ships for tasting and shelf-testing. Pack artwork and labelling requirements are agreed at this stage.

  3. Quotation & order

    Final pricing on the agreed specification, Incoterms and payment terms confirmed in writing, then a production slot is booked.

  4. Production & packing

    The run is baked, cooled, date-coded and packed to your carton configuration, with labels checked against the approved artwork.

  5. Shipment

    Goods move to Karachi for sea freight, with documents released against your terms and tracking shared through to arrival.
