Who we are

A Multan factory built for the trade, not the shelf.

Danan Foods is a Pakistani food manufacturer based on Bahawalpur Road, Multan. We produce biscuits, wafers, cup cakes, nimko and candies for the local trade — the kiryana store, the general store, the canteen and the tea stall that makes up most of how Pakistan actually buys a biscuit.

That means our job is not to design a pretty pack for a supermarket aisle. It is to bake consistently, hold a shelf life, keep the price point honest and make sure a carton lands where it was ordered. Every rupee pack leaving this factory carries the same promise: a good biscuit, at a price that makes sense.

From the same plant we serve export buyers in the UAE, Madagascar and Zimbabwe, and support private-label partners who need a manufacturer that can hold a specification.

How we make it

From flour intake to sealed carton.

Four stages, one continuous chain of custody. Nothing leaves the gate without a batch code, a packing date and a quality sign-off.

01

Sourcing & intake

Flour, sugar, oils and milk powder are checked on arrival — moisture, smell, packaging integrity — before anything is released to the mixing floor.

02

Mixing & forming

Recipes are followed by weight, not by eye. Dough rests, then goes through rotary moulders that set the shape and the weight of every piece.

03

Baking & cooling

A travelling oven bakes to a fixed profile. Biscuits then run through a cooling tunnel so they pack without sweating into the wrapper.

04

Packing & dispatch

Flow-wrap, date code, carton, seal, stack. Cartons are counted against the order sheet before the truck is loaded.
