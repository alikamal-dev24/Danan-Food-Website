A bakery that behaves like a factory.

Danan Foods operates from a single site on Bahawalpur Road in Multan — flour in at one end, sealed cartons out at the other. Biscuits, wafers, cup cakes, nimko and candies under one roof, and one quality standard across all of them.

Who we are

Built to supply a country, not a counter.

We started where most Pakistani food businesses start: a small production setup, a handful of products, and a market that had to be earned one carton at a time. What has not changed is the way we think about the job — make a product that is worth its price, and make it the same way every single day.

Today the plant runs biscuit and cookie lines, wafer baking, cup cake production and packing for nimko and candies. Our own brands — Super Star, Coconut Delight and Butter Delight — sit alongside contract work for buyers who want a Pakistani bakery partner without building a bakery.

Most of our volume moves through distributors into general trade: the shop on the corner, the canteen, the tea stall, the school gate. That customer is price-sensitive and deeply loyal to a good biscuit. Serving them well is the whole business.

On site

One building, one chain of custody.

Raw material intake, mixing, forming, baking, cooling, packing and finished-goods storage all happen on the same site. Nothing is outsourced, and nothing sits in a third-party warehouse between the oven and the truck.

Intake

Dry store

Flour, sugar and milk powder are stored off the floor, away from walls, and released to production only after inspection.

Production

Baking hall

Mixers, rotary moulders and travelling ovens run to a fixed recipe and a fixed temperature profile.

Out

Packing & dispatch

Flow-wrap, date code, carton and stack — counted against the order sheet before loading.

How we work

Three things we do not compromise.

01

Consistency

The same recipe, the same weight, the same bake. A customer who buys the same pack twice should not be able to tell the difference.

02

Price discipline

Our products are built to a price the general trade can actually sell at. Value is the product, not a discount campaign.

03

Reliability

Orders confirmed are orders dispatched. Distributors plan their month around us, and that only works if we keep the promise.

Quality

Traceable from the batch back.

Every run is dated and coded. If a carton is questioned in the market, we can trace the batch to the day it was baked, the materials it was made from and the shift that packed it.
